# Appearance

Liam looks older than he is. His hair is gray

# Personality

Liam is a gruff man who doesn't have time for fooling around.

# Relationships

- [[Stuart]] (Subordinate, ~~Twink~~)

# Interactions

Liam was found by [[The Party]] in [[Shadows Over Ironspire 5]]. He and [[Stuart]] were the only survivors after [[The Crimson Fist]] attacked the [[Western Watchtower]] where they were stationed.

Liam gave [[Tim]] a [[Sending Stone]] to communicate, then sent [[The Party]] back to [[Ironspire]] to get reinforcements. On their way back, [[The Crimson Fist]] returned and killed Liam.
