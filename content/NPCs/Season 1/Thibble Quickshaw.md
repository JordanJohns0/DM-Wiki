**Thibble Quickshaw** was a eccentric artificer who has spent his life researching mind flayers. The research as turned into a bit of an obsession over the years.

He constantly wears a Ring of Mind Shielding that he crafted himself, believing the mind flayers to be everywhere, constantly observing and waiting for the right chance to strike.

Thibble is a valued member of [[The Bronze Anvil]] guild. He has invented quite a few valuable machines (See [[Thibble Quickshaw#Inventions|Inventions]]).

## Inventions

Thibble has invented a fair number of creations in his days. This is a non-comprehensive list:

- The Mechanical Flumph
- Steam-Powered Excavator
- Clockwork Courier
  - A Mechanical, Rat-Like creature. It has a compartment in its back, and can deliver messages to anyone within [[Ironspire]].
  - These creatures have become incredibly popular, and it is rare to not see one while walking through the streets of the city.
- Eyes of Minute Seeing
- Psionic Shield Matrix (See Below)

# Psionic Shield Matrix

The Psionic Shield Matrix is a device that can be setup in one's home in order to prevent Illithid thought detection and invasion.

- Most people don't need this, until Thibble created the Gearwrought Illithid and had them raid town. This caused a mass panic, and made Thibble very wealthy.

# Thibble's Story

## Early Life

Thibble grew up an outcast among his peers in the Deep Gnome colony of [[Quarvex]]. In his younger years, he stumbled upon a dying mind flayer, left for dead by its colony. Instead of fearing the creature, Thibble's insatiable curiosity took hold. He studied the mind flayer, and in its dying moments, he learned secrets about its anatomy, its society, and its goals.

Haunted by this encounter, Thibble became obsessed with the idea of recreating these enigmatic creatures but under the control of the deep gnomes. He envisioned a city guarded by mechanical illithids, ensuring the safety of his people from the horrors of the Underdark.

## The Destruction of Quarvex

Thibble struggled to come close to what he wanted to create, until he discovered [[The Elemental Shards#Shard of Ember|Shard of Ember]] With the shard's power, he created the prototype [[Gearwrought Illithid|Gearwrought Illithids]]. With this invention, he was eager to demonstrate its power and usefulness to the city's leaders. He planned a showcase, using a controlled environment within the city’s borders. However, things went horribly wrong.

The Gearwrought Illithids, running on the raw power of the Ember Shard, went haywire. They started to malfunction, and their unstable energy cores began detonating, causing widespread destruction. Buildings crumbled, fires broke out, and in a matter of hours, Quarvex lay in ruins.

Thibble managed to escape, and was the only survivor. At first, he was horrified by what he had caused. However, he saw the potential and power the Ember Shard possessed. He created power cores that could handle the Shard and eventually created Illithids that were stable.

## The Creation of Il'kessath

Thibble emerged from the depths of the Underdark, the weight of Quarvex's devastation on his shoulders. At Ironspire, he recounted tales of Quarvex's fall to local settlements, not as a result of his miscalculated inventions but of a horrifying invasion of mind flayers. He painted a picture of an overwhelming force that left no survivors.

Naming the now-ruined city of Quarvex as "Il'Kessath," he ensured that any adventurers or curious souls would be deterred from visiting, fearing the hive of mind flayers he had described. This dual purpose of renaming served both as a memorial to the city he once loved and as a protective measure to keep the curious away from his misdeeds.
