**Race:** Dwarf
**Class:** Artificer

Torvin is a member of the [[The Illuminated Archive]] and the creator of the [[Cerebral Conduit]], a device that allows the user to meld with a mind flayer, granting untold knowledge.

using the Cerebral Conduit turns one eye of the user a solid, inky black, tainting them with the [[Mark of the Mind Flayer]]. Continued use of this device causes the user to become corrupted and turn into a [[Mind Flayer Thrall]].

In the cast of Torvin, he has been kidnapping citizens of [[Thyrsia]] and giving them to [[Stilgar Giriou]] to convert into [[Mind Flayer Thrall]]s.
