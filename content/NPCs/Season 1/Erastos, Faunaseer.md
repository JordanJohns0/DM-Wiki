Erastos is a Faunaseer, which is an occupation that involves researching and learning about animals. Erastos is a collector, devoting his life to finding and preserving animals that are fantastical, rare, or otherwise endangered.

## Living Arrangements

Erastos lives in the forest north of [[Thyrsia]], in a humble home he built himself. He has various cages of all sizes surrounding his home, containing all sorts of creatures. He feeds them all and treats them all well.

## Creatures in his possession

- Laconian Fish
  - A silver-scaled fish with the ability to swim through the air as if it were water.
- Nymph Nectar Bee
  - These bees produce honey that has magical properties when consumed. The bees themselves are iridescent and quite beautiful.
- Stymphalian Chick
  - A bird species known for their metal feathers and sharp beaks. They are dangerous when fully grown but this one is a small chick and its feathers are still soft.
- Gorgon Snakes
  - About the size of a common snake, they do not have the petrifying gaze of Gorgon Serpent, their larger counterparts.
- Fey Mouse
  - A regular mouse that is glowing a faint green colour. It possesses minor magical abilities, such as creating tiny sparks or a puff of smoke.
- Elysium Butterfly
  - A beautiful, glowing butterfly that supposedly only exists in the Elysium Fields. It's a marvel to look at and is said to bring good luck.
- **Blink Dog**
  - A personal favourite of Erastos. She follows him around wherever he goes.
  - Her name is Zephyr.
- Mana Wisp
  - A small, floating orb of magical energy. It's harmless but tends to drain magic from spells cast near it.
- Glass Frog
  - This small amphibian has transparent skin, making its internal organs visible. It's a strange and beautiful sight.
- Ice Mephit
  - A small, impish creature composed of ice and snow. It's capable of creating tiny snowstorms around it.
