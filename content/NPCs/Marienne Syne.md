# Interactions

## [[Shadows Over Ironspire 2]]

Marienne is first seen arguing with [[Quinn Syne]] when [[The Party]] first arrives to [[Ironspire]]. [[Quinn Syne|Quinn]] wanted to go on the offensive against [[The Crimson Fist]], while Marienne thinks [[Ironspire]] does not have the resources to do so.

## [[Shadows Over Ironspire 3]]

Marienne gives [[The Party]] a quest. She asks them to go to the [[Western Watchtower]] and check in with the guards stationed there since they missed the deadline to report it.

## [[Shadows Over Ironspire 6]]

[[The Party]] returns to [[Ironspire]] from the quest and reports to Marienne, who was in the middle of an argument with [[Quinn Syne|Quinn]] and [[Sylvester Syne|Sylvester]]. They tell her that the guards have been ambushed and the two survivors, [[Liam]] and [[Stuart]], both died after [[the party]] left the [[Western Watchtower]].

Marienne's stance was, "We don't have the numbers to fight them! We need to ask [[The Eldritch Order]] for help." When she said this, [[Quinn Syne|Quinn]] and [[Sylvester Syne|Sylvester]] were shocked that the idea was even suggested.
