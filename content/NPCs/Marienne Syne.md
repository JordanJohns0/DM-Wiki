# Interactions

Marienne is first seen arguing with [[Quinn Syne]] when the party first arrives to [[Ironspire]] in [[Shadows Over Ironspire 2]]. [[Quinn Syne|Quinn]] wanted to go on the offensive against [[The Crimson Fist]], while Marienne thinks [[Ironspire]] does not have the resources to do so.

Marienne gives the party a quest in [[Shadows Over Ironspire 3]]. She asks them to go to the [[Western Watchtower]] and check in with the guards stationed there since they missed the deadline to report it.
