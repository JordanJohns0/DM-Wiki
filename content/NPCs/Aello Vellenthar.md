Aello is the seemingly mild-mannered librarian at the [[Starlight Library]] and is a respected figure in [[Thyrsia]]. She is a half-elf of middling years with silver-streaked auburn hair typically tied in a loose bun. Her spectacles, always perched precariously on the tip of her nose, magnify her sharp, perceptive eyes. She always seems to wear an air of knowing more than she lets on.

Aello is one of the key figures within [[The Illuminated Archive]]. She is a powerful divination wizard, using her magic to guide the Archive's efforts and ensure the protection of the knowledge they guard.
