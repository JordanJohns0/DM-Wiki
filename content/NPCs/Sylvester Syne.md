# Interactions

Sylvester is the successor to [[Thibble Quickshaw]] after [[Thibble Quickshaw|Thibble]] was executed.

## [[Shadows Over Ironspire 6]]

Sylvester was in an argument with [[Marienne Syne|Marienne]] and [[Quinn Syne|Quinn]] when [[The Party]] returned from their quest that [[Marienne Syne|Marienne]] gave them. They were arguing about what their course of action should be against [[The Crimson Fist]]. Sylvester's stance was "The hobgoblins would never dare to attack our city; even if they do, nothing bad will come of it."
