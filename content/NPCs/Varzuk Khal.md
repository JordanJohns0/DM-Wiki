# Personality

**Varzuk** has a brilliant strategic mind and charismatic speaker, capable of getting armies of Hobgoblins to fight for him.

# Goals

**Varzuk** wants to resurrect [[Todorian the Devourer]] using the [[Dragon Resurrection Ritual]]. To do this, he needs to conquer [[Ironspire]] to get access to the ancient battleground where [[Todorian the Devourer|Todorian]] was slain.
