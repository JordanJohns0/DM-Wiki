# Overview

The Western Watchtower is an outpost for the [[Ironspire City Guard]]. It is in ruin, and most of the tower has crumbled long ago. There is a staircase that goes about 1/2 of the way up to the where the top used to be, and that can still serve as a lookout.

It stands at a crucial spot in the area, and the people of [[Ironspire]] really want to hold the position.

Currently it is occupied by [[The Crimson Fist]] after they killed the [[Ironspire]] guards that were stationed here.

# Important NPCs

- [[Liam]]
- [[Stuart]]

# Interactions

The party was given a quest to go here from [[Marienne Syne|Marienne]] in [[Shadows Over Ironspire 3]].

The party arrived here in [[Shadows Over Ironspire 4]], finding it overrun with goblins from [[The Crimson Fist]]. The party killed them, and found [[Liam]] and [[Stuart]] tied up as the only survivors from [[Ironspire]].
