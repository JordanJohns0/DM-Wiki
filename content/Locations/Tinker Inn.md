# Overview

The Tinker Inn is a steampunk-themed tavern and inn. It has animated machines and gears on the walls, and it is very warm and humid inside due to the steam power they use.

# Interactions

[[The Party]] visited during [[Shadows Over Ironspire 3]]. [[Persephone]] and [[James Blackburn]] both bought an entire gallon of Ale and drink them using swirly straws.
