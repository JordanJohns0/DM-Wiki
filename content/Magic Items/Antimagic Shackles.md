*Two braces made of black steel that go around the wrist of the victim. While attached, they slowly and softly pulse with psychic energy*

While attached to a creature, that creature cannot cast spells or use magical abilities. 

**Break Free - Action:** Once during of of their turns, the creature may make a DC 17 check of whatever the creature's spellcasting ability is in order to destroy the shackles. 
- If a PC fails this check, they take 1d6 psychic damage per level the character is. 
- If a NPC fails it, they take 1d6 psychic damage per CR the creature is, to a minimum of 1d6.
