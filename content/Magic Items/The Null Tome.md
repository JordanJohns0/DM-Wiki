# Description

The contents of this blasphemous manuscript are the substance of corruption and erasure. It contains knowledge so profoundly wrong that even to perceive its pages is to feel reality loosen its grip.

Most believe the tome was not authored in any mortal sense. It is instead to be a creation of The Silent One, recorded by unknown hands that could not resist transcribing what should not be expressed. Within its pages are thoughts that should not be spoken aloud and magic that replaces meaning with absence.

# Properties

When a creature that is neither a Fiend nor Undead attunes to the tome, that creature must succeed on a DC 17 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature does not attune and becomes mute.

A creature attuned to the book must spend 10 hours studying it to learn a spell. The spells are learned in the below order, and the book can be studied until no spells remain.

1. [[Unlearn Language]]
2. [Banishment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618906-banishment)
3. [Power Word: Silence](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/502409-power-word-silence)
