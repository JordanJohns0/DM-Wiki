Wondrous Item, uncommon

---

While wearing these dark lenses, you have [Darkvision](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Darkvision) out to 30 feet. If you already have Darkvision, wearing these goggles increases its range by 30 feet.
