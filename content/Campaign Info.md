### **Main Plot:**
- [[The Crimson Fist]] are attacking [[Ironspire]] and want their land
	- [[Ironspire]] wants to bargain with [[The Crimson Fist]] to avoid conflict. The council doesn't want to give emergency war powers to the old man [[Sylvester Syne]].
	- [[The Crimson Fist]] will succeed unless the leadership of [[Ironspire]] can make a deal with [[Mouthless]] to help fend them off. [[Varzuk Khal]] is already trying to make a deal with him for himself.
		- If [[The Eldritch Order]] joins the war (need to be convinced), they would use rituals to summon demons to help whichever side they join, rather than fight themselves.
