# Overview

The **[[Ironspire]] City Guard** is a dedicated force of professionals tasked with maintaining peace and security within the bustling steampunk metropolis. Given the city's innovative nature, the guards themselves are often equipped with some of the latest advancements from [[The Bronze Anvil]] Guild.

## Equipment

Standard issue equipment for the guards includes:

- Ironspire Guard Uniform: a suit of protective leather dyed in the city's colors, complete with brass fittings and decorative gears
- A longsword
- A specially designed compact crossbow that uses a built-in gear mechanism to quickly load and fire bolts.

Specialized equipment the City Guard may use:

- [[Watcher's Eyes]]
- [[Springstep Boots]]
