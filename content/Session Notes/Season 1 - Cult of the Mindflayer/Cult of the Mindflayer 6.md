## Cultist Room
- A Dark, Cold room with a masked cultist kneeling in the center of it. The windows are open, a draft makes the curtains flow. Candles are placed around the room, lighting the corners in dim light. A fireplace crackles softly off to the side. There is a baby cradle and a rocking chair.
	- When interacted with, the cultist starts whispering and ambient whispers fill the room and grow louder and louder. Shadows grow darker, candles flicker. 
	- The fireplace is extinguished with a sudden hiss, leaving the room in near darkness. The door slams shut behind you. 
	- Purple light begins to glow behind the door and from the fireplace, as well as the cradle and the rocking chair. Suddenly, the door flies open to reveal a void with many arms grasping out of it. Similar arms begin reaching out of the fireplace, cradle, and rocking chair
	- Cultist says, "My dreaming ends... your nightmare... begins..." then a shadowy figure erupts from him, hovering above, chains rattling. Roll initiative
