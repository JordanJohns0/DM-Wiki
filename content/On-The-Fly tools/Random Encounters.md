# [[The Underdark]]
**Non-Combat:**
1. **Bioluminescent Cavern:** The party stumbles upon a cavern filled with glowing fungi and crystals, casting a serene and beautiful light throughout. They could potentially harvest some of these for light sources.
2. **Lost Myconid:** A confused Myconid sprout approaches the party, having been separated from its circle. It seeks assistance to find its way home.
3. **Underground River:** The party comes across a swift-moving underground river with something glimmering in the depths. To cross or investigate, they'll need to find a safe method or risk the treacherous waters.
4. **Derro Pilgrimage:** A group of Derro, normally hostile but now on a religious pilgrimage, cross the party's path. They're in a trance-like state, and any interaction could disrupt their ritual.
5. **Cave-In:** Part of the tunnel collapses behind the party, cutting off their way back. They'll need to find a new route or figure out how to clear the debris.
6. **Abandoned Campsite:** The party discovers an old campsite with belongings scattered around. There's no sign of the previous inhabitants, but a journal remains, detailing their exploration of a nearby hidden treasure.
7. **Echoing Whispers:** The cave system echoes with mysterious whispers, potentially leading the party to a group of benevolent ghosts who have a story to share or a message to deliver.
8. **Underdark Bazaar:** The party encounters a hidden underground market, where various Underdark denizens trade in rare fungi, gems, and other strange items.
9. **Unstable Ground:** A section of the cave floor is unstable. If the party isn't careful, they could fall into a lower chamber filled with strange, harmless but curious creatures.
10. **Maze of Crystals:** A chamber filled with prismatic crystals reflects and refracts light in disorienting patterns. Navigating through will require intelligence and wit.

**Combat:**
1. **Hook Horror Hunt:** A Hook Horror and its young are hunting in the same area as the party, and they might see the adventurers as prey or threats to their nest.
2. **Quaggoth Patrol:** A group of Quaggoth, enslaved by a local Drow faction, is patrolling the area. They'll attack on sight but might be reasoned with if the party realizes their enslaved state.
3. **Mind Flayer Scout:** A lone Mind Flayer scout, assessing the region for colonization, confronts the party. It might have a few Intellect Devourers as companions.
4. **Ambush by Drow:** A Drow raiding party tries to ambush the adventurers, intending to capture them for their spider goddess.
5. **Giant Cave Toads:** The party accidentally stumbles into the territory of a few giant, aggressive cave toads.
6. **Fungus Infestation:** A chamber filled with zombified creatures controlled by Yellow Mold or another aggressive fungus.
7. **Duergar Thieves:** A group of Duergar thieves, using their invisibility to their advantage, tries to rob the party and will resort to violence if caught.
8. **Oozing Danger:** A Black Pudding or Gelatinous Cube blocks a narrow passage the party needs to traverse.
9. **Chuul Nest:** The waters of an underground lake are home to a Chuul, which sees the party as potential food for its young.
10. **Troglodyte Ambush:** Drawn by the scent of the party, a group of Troglodytes tries to ambush them from a series of small tunnels and caves.
