# The Drunken Minotaur Murders
The Velvet Hand is investigating a conflict that occurred outside of [[The Drunken Minotaur]]. They know:
- 4 People have been murdered gruesomely. 
- 1 Person had their right hand removed.
- People in the tavern overhead the victims talking about [[The Illuminated Archive]]. Nobody seems to know what that is, though.
