The party stopped a [[The Crimson Fist|Crimson Fist]] attack on an innocent man, [[Gilbert]], and saved his life. They are going to continue on their way to [[Ironspire]] to protect  [[Nolan]]'s caravan.

# Chapter 1 - After the battle
The party interacts with [[Gilbert]] after they saved him from the hobgoblin raid. 
## Scene - His Thanks
[[Gilbert]] gives the party his thanks after he was saved. 
- "I've lost m' home and would've lost m' life too if you didn't help just now."
- "I don't have much in the way of coin to offer, but I can offer you something for the trouble."
He rummages through his cart
- "I was goin' to try and sell these when I got to town, but I never woulda made it there if not for you."
and finds a sack, then hands it over. Inside is:
- A worn book, written in Abyssal. It has 2 spell scrolls, [Arcane Lock](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618868-arcane-lock) and [Crown of Madness](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619056-crown-of-madness)
- A small wooden box, inside is a single polished iron ingot.
- A single copper piece
- A trowel
- A stale loaf of bread
## Scene - Resting
[[Gilbert]] sits down on the grass, leaning against his cart and lets out a big sigh and closes his eyes.
- "After all that commotion I think I need a wee bit o' rest before I make the rest o' the journey."
## Scene - Back to the Caravan
The party goes back to the caravan. When they return, [[Nolan]] asks what the commotion was.
- "You didn't offer help, did you? I don't work for free."




# Chapter 2 - Ironspire
The party arrives at Ironspire.
## Scene - The Gates of Ironspire
The caravan rounds the final bend in the mountain road. [[Ironspire]] rises ahead, dark stone walls pressed against the cliffs, blue banners hanging still in the cold air. The gate stands open, but a crowd has gathered just inside. 

As the caravan approaches, raised voices carry across the stones.
